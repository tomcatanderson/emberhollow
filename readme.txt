Potion & Dominion: The Alchemist of Emberhollow

You are Jon, a wandering traveler and aspiring alchemist who arrives in the fog-shrouded steampunk-medieval town of Emberhollow with little more than a satchel of basic tools, a few dried herbs, and a hunger for power. The town is small, self-contained, and ripe for influence—its gears and steam pipes clank alongside thatched roofs and cobblestone streets. Your goal is not just to brew potions, but to systematically take over Emberhollow and build a personal harem of loyal companions through a mix of commerce, influence, romance, and carefully applied alchemy.Core Gameplay Loop (Stardew-style with deeper systems)
Grow, gather, harvest: Cultivate herbs, rare plants, fungi, and alchemical reagents in your garden plots and greenhouse. Forage in the surrounding woods, abandoned mines, and steam-vent caves. Some ingredients must first be refined or created (distilled essences, fermented bases, crystallized extracts) before they can be used in higher-tier recipes.

Brew & experiment: Combine up to 4 ingredients in your brewing station. Effects are not always predictable—experimentation unlocks new recipes, variants, and side effects. Successful discoveries are permanently added to your codex.

Sell, upgrade, expand: Run a potion shop from your residence. Earn gold, reputation, and influence. Upgrade your home (more rooms, better workshop, private chambers), brewing equipment (higher purity, larger batches, automated stills), and garden infrastructure.
Influence & takeover: Use potions, gifts, favors, information, and romance to shift the balance of power in town. Gain control of businesses, key institutions, and eventually the town itself. Loyalty and desire from your harem provide both mechanical bonuses and narrative control.
Potion Types (examples)Love potions and attraction oils, sleeping draughts, pain relievers and healing salves, massage oils and aphrodisiacs, stimulants/amphetamines, narcotics and euphorics, alcohols and fermented spirits, memory fog, truth serums, strength enhancers, and more experimental or dangerous compounds. Many have multiple tiers and can be tailored for specific targets or combined effects.

Setting: Semi-medieval with steampunk elements: brass pipes, clockwork mechanisms, steam-powered elevators and irrigation, alchemical forges, and airship docking platforms on the outskirts. The town sits in a valley rich in unique flora that thrives near geothermal vents.

Notes on the system
Asterisked items (*) are intermediate products that must be crafted first (oils, essences, concentrates, extracts).
Experimentation can produce variant effects, side effects, or entirely new recipes.
Quality of ingredients, brewing equipment upgrades, and player skill affect potency, duration, and side-effect severity.
Many of the stronger love, narcotic, and influence potions have ethical and social consequences that affect relationships and town reputation.
This gives you a deep, expandable alchemy system that supports both the cozy farming/brewing loop and the darker influence/harem/takeover fantasy.

Shared Relationship Systems
Every romanceable woman has two persistent meters:

Relationship / Affection (0–100): Tracks emotional bond, trust, and willingness to deepen the connection. Raised by gifts, quests, dialogue choices, shared activities, and successful potion use. High values unlock exclusive scenes, live-in status, and harem integration.
Wetness / Arousal (0–100): Tracks immediate physical readiness. Raised by erotic dialogue, touching, certain oils/potions, visual stimuli, and successful prior encounters. At high values the character becomes more receptive, vocal, and physically responsive. It decays over time if not acted upon, but can be re-stimulated.

Love potions, aphrodisiac oils, and certain narcotics can artificially inflate either meter (or both). Overuse creates dependency, reduced agency, and long-term personality shifts.
Male obstacles are handled through three broad approaches:

Appease: Gifts, favors, reputation boosts, or mild beneficial potions that make them supportive or indifferent.
Entrap: Addiction (narcotics, alcohol, stimulants), blackmail, financial dependence, or social ruin that removes their ability or will to interfere.
Remove: More extreme options (framing, exile, permanent chemical control, or engineered “accidents”). These carry higher risk to town reputation and can lock certain story paths.
